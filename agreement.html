Sibling Agreement

A personal commitment between siblings

Before entering this agreement, please answer these honestly. There are no wrong answers — but the agreement should only be signed if you genuinely mean it.

1. If life gets difficult, will you stand by your sibling?

2. If other family members disagree with your sibling, will you still hear your sibling out and support them?

3. Will you keep this commitment even when you are angry with each other?

Please answer all three questions. The agreement only opens when all three answers are YES.

This certifies that Afsheen Jahan Nujha voluntarily agrees to the following sibling commitment.

🤝 The Commitment

  1. We are a team. Whatever happens between us, we will remember that we are siblings and will make a genuine effort not to turn against each other.
  2. We stand by each other. When one of us is struggling, we will try to support the other rather than leaving them alone when they need family.
  3. Even when others disagree. If another family member disagrees with one of us, we will not automatically abandon our sibling. We will listen, understand the situation, and give each other a fair chance to speak.
  4. No blind support but. Standing by each other does not mean supporting something harmful. It means remaining family, being honest, and helping each other make better decisions but if it is about his/her life and emotions then it should be supported.
  5. Arguments do not cancel family. We can be angry, disagree, or need space. But temporary conflict will not be treated as permission to permanently abandon the relationship.
  6. Respect stays. No matter how serious the disagreement, we will try to avoid humiliation, unnecessary insults, or deliberately exposing private matters.
  7. Keep private things private. Personal conversations and vulnerabilities shared between us should be protected and not used against each other.
  8. Show up when it matters. When the situation is genuinely important, we will make a reasonable effort to be present, communicate, and help.
  9. This goes both ways. The same loyalty, respect, honesty, and support expected from one sibling is promised to the other.
  10. Long-term promise. Circumstances, opinions, and people may change. The intention of this agreement is that our sibling bond remains something we protect and value.
